How to Use

Choose how you take part.

Organization For observers trained by a cooperative, extension office, school or research institute. Enter your organization code, then record. Individual For growers taking part on their own. A short lesson and quiz (about 10 minutes), then record.

Organizations

An organization acts as the coordinator for its growers: it carries out outreach, teaches the guideline, issues observer IDs and collects records. To register an organization and receive a code, open a request on the project tracker.

Researchers

No sign-up is needed. Use Search and Data.
