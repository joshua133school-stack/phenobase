How submission works in this release. The online upload server is not running yet. Observations you enter here are kept on this device only. When you have finished, press Download CSV and send the file to your national coordinator (or attach it to an issue on the project tracker). The form works without an internet connection once the page has loaded.

Read the observation guideline first. Fields marked * are required. Use one entry per stage. For example, full flowering and end of flowering are two entries.
