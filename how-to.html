How to Use

Farmers

  1. Read the guideline in your language: English | 한국어 | 日本語 | 简体中文 (all languages).
  2. Mark 5 trees of one cultivar, or a fixed plot of about 10 plants in the middle of the field.
  3. Look every 1–2 days while buds open, flowers open and fruit ripens; weekly otherwise.
  4. Write the first date each stage is reached on the recording sheet. Stages and their codes: Stage codes.
  5. Enter the dates in the Submit form (works offline on a phone) and send the CSV file to your coordinator.

Researchers

  1. Search by crop, country, stage and years. The page address is a permanent link to the query.
  2. Choose the view: record list, summary by year, or summary by site with trend.
  3. Download results as CSV, or the whole data set from Data.
  4. Read the field dictionary and cite as shown under Licence.

Partners

  • Translate the guideline: translation status.
  • Become a national node or farmer group: About → How to join.
