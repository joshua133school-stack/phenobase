2. Lessons

2.1 Choose your plants

Observe the same marked plants every visit and, for trees, every year. Avoid edges, walls, sick or newly planted plants.

2.2 When to look

Every day or every second day while buds open, flowers open, ears appear and fruit ripens. Once a week at other times. Look at the whole tree or plot, sunny and shady sides.

2.3 Deciding the date

Record the first day the stage is true for your marked plants as a group.

Firstseen on at least one marked plant
Beginningabout 1 in 10
Full / halfabout half or more
Endalmost all

Missed a visit? Give your best estimate, mark it low confidence and write the last "not yet" and first "already" dates in the notes. Never copy a neighbour's date.

2.4 Your core stages

Always record these. The code goes with every record.

All stages: Stage codes.

2.5 Photos and notes

If you can, take a photo of the whole tree or plot and one close-up, from the same place each time, with no faces. Write frost, hail, typhoon, pruning, greenhouse or flower-induction treatments in the notes with the date.

3. Quiz

Answer all questions correctly to continue. You can try again as often as you like.
