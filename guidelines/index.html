Farmer phenology observation guideline

Guideline PR-G-01 tells farmers how to choose plants, when to look, and how to decide the date of each stage. It is one document, translated into the languages of the region. The English text is the reference version; where a translation and the English text differ, the English text applies until the translation is reviewed.

Each guideline ends with a recording sheet that can be printed and kept in the orchard or field.

How to translate

  1. Open an issue on the project tracker with the label translation and the language name, so that work is not duplicated.
  2. Copy guidelines/en.html to guidelines/<language code>.html and translate the text. Keep the section numbers, stage codes and table structure unchanged.
  3. Use the farmers' own words for stages (for example the local word for "full bloom"), not only textbook terms. Where a local term is used, keep the BBCH code beside it.
  4. Translate cultivar examples into local cultivars that farmers in your country know.
  5. The translation becomes reviewed when a second person, a native-speaking agronomist or extension officer, has checked it and five farmers have tested it in the field.
